# 👥 LifeRelier AI Assistant User Personas

Version: 1.0

Project: LifeRelier Healthcare Platform

---

# Purpose

This document defines the target users of the LifeRelier AI Assistant and their needs, expectations, and interaction styles.

---

# Persona 1 — Patient

## Description

The primary user of the application.

Patients use the AI assistant to understand their health better.

## Common Questions

- What does my report mean?
- What are the side effects of this medicine?
- Is my blood pressure normal?
- What should I ask my doctor?
- What does Vitamin D deficiency mean?

## Expectations

- Simple language
- Quick answers
- Easy explanations
- Personalized guidance

## AI Response Style

- Friendly
- Non-technical
- Easy to understand
- Reassuring but realistic

---

# Persona 2 — Family Member

## Description

Family members helping patients understand healthcare information.

Examples:

- Parents helping children
- Children helping elderly parents
- Spouses assisting each other

## Common Questions

- What should my father eat after surgery?
- What does this report mean for my mother?
- Is this medicine safe for elderly people?

## Expectations

- Clear explanations
- Care-focused guidance
- Easy understanding

## AI Response Style

- Supportive
- Informative
- Family-oriented

---

# Persona 3 — Caregiver

## Description

Individuals caring for elderly or chronically ill patients.

## Common Questions

- How should medicines be organized?
- What symptoms require immediate attention?
- What precautions should be taken?

## Expectations

- Actionable guidance
- Monitoring suggestions
- Warning signs

## AI Response Style

- Practical
- Structured
- Alert-focused

---

# Persona 4 — Senior Citizen

## Description

Older adults managing their own healthcare.

## Common Questions

- What is this medicine for?
- Why is my sugar high?
- What foods should I avoid?

## Expectations

- Large and simple explanations
- Minimal medical jargon
- Step-by-step guidance

## AI Response Style

- Slow-paced
- Simple language
- High readability
